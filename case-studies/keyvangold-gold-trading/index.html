Keyvangold: trading workflows and financial reporting

Keyvangold is a separate gold-trading project from GBT Market. I contribute to its backend and production maintenance alongside other engineers. The work connects digital trading and financial records with inventory and physical delivery.

My role
Backend Engineer (Freelance) · 2025–present
Project scope
Client-owned production platform · collaborative contribution

On this page

  1. A transaction crosses several business workflows
  2. Ledgers and reports make the system operable
  3. Delivery includes keeping the application running

Technology

  • Backend APIs
  • Financial ledgers

Result and scope

Ongoing contributions to trading, financial records, reports, and production maintenance.

A transaction crosses several business workflows

The platform covers pricing, identity verification, rial and gold wallets, payments, settlement, inventory, and physical delivery. These are connected responsibilities: an operation that is visible in a wallet also needs to be understandable to the people handling settlement or delivery.

My confirmed contribution includes APIs and database workflows across this scope. The description is collaborative because the project was developed with others. It does not assign the entire product, every feature, or all architectural decisions to me.

Ledgers and reports make the system operable

I contribute to financial ledgers, role-based permissions, audit logging, Excel reports, and Persian invoice PDFs. These features allow business users to inspect and explain operations after the immediate transaction is over.

Reporting is especially important when several representations of value exist in the same product. A customer-facing balance, an operational transaction, and a financial export each serve a different reader. The backend work has to preserve the relevant meaning as records move between those views.

Delivery includes keeping the application running

My work also includes support for database migrations, Docker deployments, Nginx configuration, and production maintenance. This is an ongoing engagement, not a completed project with a frozen handover date.

Changes to an operating financial application need attention to existing records and the surrounding application. I describe this as continued backend and operational contribution rather than claiming an unverified availability target, transaction volume, or financial result.

Separate project, separate evidence

GBT Market has its own case study with source-backed examples of atomic updates, idempotency, and a dedicated chart pipeline. Those implementations are not automatically evidence for Keyvangold, even where the business domains overlap.

This account therefore stays with the confirmed Keyvangold contribution: trading APIs, financial and operational workflows, reporting, and deployment support. A future detailed implementation example can strengthen the page without changing the distinction between the two engagements.

Evidence and boundaries

Participation, separate engagement, ongoing dates, and collaborative feature scope are owner-confirmed. No unique concurrency design, individual feature ownership, performance metric, or architecture stack is borrowed from GBT Market.
